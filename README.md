## Hi, I'm Sebastiano

I'm a dual M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Chicago and Mechatronic Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, graduating in May 2027. I work on robots, mostly where electronics meets software: ROS 2 nodes for localization and planning, and the boards that run on our competition robots in Team RoboTO.

I'm looking for robotics internships for summer 2027.

### Projects

- **[TurtleBot3 navigation and localization with ROS 2](https://github.com/ssebba/SESASR)**: reactive obstacle avoidance, an Extended Kalman Filter that fuses odometry, IMU and AprilTag landmarks, and a Dynamic Window Approach planner that follows a moving target. Tuned in Gazebo, then run on the real robot.
- **[DRL obstacle avoidance](https://github.com/ssebba/DRL-Obstacle-avoidance)**: a Double DQN agent in TensorFlow that drives a TurtleBot3 through cluttered maps using only LiDAR, with a shaped reward that keeps it away from walls.
- **Team RoboTO electronics**: the robot's main controller board (3 CAN buses, RS-485, USB) and an IMU board designed in KiCad, plus the IMU firmware. Hardware details are in my portfolio, available on request.
